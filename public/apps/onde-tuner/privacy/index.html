OndeTuner Privacy Policy

Publisher: Free River House / onde.surf · Product: OndeTuner (com.onde.tuner) · Version 1.0.0 · Updated 2026-09-17

Summary

OndeTuner is an offline-first guitar and bass tuner for iPhone and Apple Watch. Pitch detection runs entirely on your device using the microphone. We do not create accounts, do not collect personal data, do not run analytics or advertising SDKs, and do not sell or share data with third parties.

What we access

  • Microphone: Used only while the app (or Watch app) is open and tuning, to measure pitch. Audio is processed on-device and is not uploaded or retained by us as identifiable recordings.
  • On-device preferences: Theme, instrument/tuning, A4 reference, and similar settings stay on your device (UserDefaults / AppStorage).

What we do not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising networks
  • No in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads in v1 (the app is free)
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No sale of personal information
  • No network required for tuning

Children

OndeTuner does not target children for data collection. Age rating intent: 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Apple App Privacy

For App Store Connect, the accurate declaration for this build is Data Not Collected, provided the shipping binary still contains no analytics/account/network telemetry. Microphone use is a permission for on-device processing, not data collected by Free River House.

Contact

Privacy: privacy@onde.surf · Support: onde.surf/ondetuner/support

Changes

If we add accounts, analytics, IAP, or any data collection, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy answers before shipping that change.

OndeTuner — Informativa privacy

Editore: Free River House / onde.surf · Prodotto: OndeTuner (com.onde.tuner) · Versione 1.0.0 · Aggiornata 2026-09-17

In sintesi

OndeTuner è un accordatore per chitarra e basso, offline-first, per iPhone e Apple Watch. Il rilevamento dell’intonazione avviene solo sul dispositivo tramite microfono. Non creiamo account, non raccogliamo dati personali, non usiamo analytics né pubblicità, e non vendiamo né condividiamo dati con terzi.

Cosa accediamo

  • Microfono: solo mentre l’app (o Watch) è aperta e stai accordando. L’audio è elaborato on-device e non viene caricato sul cloud.
  • Preferenze locali: tema, strumento/accordatura, A4 e simili restano sul tuo dispositivo.

Cosa non facciamo

  • Nessun account o login
  • Nessuna analytics o pubblicità
  • Nessun IAP, abbonamento o annuncio in v1 (app gratuita)
  • Nessun tracking tra app o siti
  • Nessuna vendita di dati
  • Nessuna rete necessaria per accordare

Minori

Classificazione età prevista: 4+. Non raccogliamo dati personali.

Privacy App Store

Dichiarazione corretta: Dati non raccolti, se il binario resta senza analytics/account/telemetria.

Contatti
